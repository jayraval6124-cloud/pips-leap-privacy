Pip's Leap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Pip's Leap (package com.pipsleap.game, "the game") handles information. The game does not require an account and does not ask you for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, seeds, outfits, purchases you made and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

In-app purchases (buying seeds)

Seeds are the in-game currency and can be bought with real money through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google may share purchase information with us (for example, which product was bought and whether it succeeded) so the game can give you your seeds. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Seeds are for use inside the game only and have no cash value. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

Rewarded ads (optional)

You can choose to watch a video ad to earn free seeds. Ads are shown only when you tap the button; they never interrupt play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See how Google uses data from ads.

Other permissions and tracking

The game does not use analytics or tracking services of its own, does not access your location, camera, microphone or contacts, and does not share your information with anyone except as described above (Google Play and Google AdMob).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We keep no personal data about you. To remove the data stored on your device, clear the game's storage or uninstall it. For ad-related data held by Google, use your Google account's ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
