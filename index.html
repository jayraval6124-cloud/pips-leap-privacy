Pip's Leap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Pip's Leap (package com.pipsleap.game) is an offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

Data on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, seeds, outfits and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Internet, accounts and permissions

The game does not need an internet connection, does not ask for any Android permissions, and has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if ads or purchases are added in a future version, this page will be updated.
